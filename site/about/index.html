The Pinus is made for you

A home in the
heart of pines

The Pinus Resort is a handful of pine-wood cottages at Nunwan, on the edge of Pahalgam, built for travellers who want the mountains without the rush. Warm rooms lined in pine, an open lawn that faces the peaks, and a team that looks after the small things so you can notice the big ones.

We keep it small on purpose. It means we know who is staying, what time you set out for Aru, and whether you would like the fire lit tonight. Comfort of home, with the thrill of the wild just outside the door.

Our way of hosting

Mehmaan Nawazi

In Kashmir, a guest is family for as long as they stay. That spirit, mehmaan nawazi, is the heart of how we host.

It shows in small, everyday ways: a warm welcome after a long drive from Srinagar, a straight answer when you ask which valley is best this week, a room that is ready when you walk in, and a team that remembers how you take your tea.

When you ask for something, our answer starts with yes. If it cannot be done, we will tell you honestly and find the next best thing. Our motto says it simply: a home in the heart of pines. We hope the charm of the cottages makes a home in your heart too.

Our spaces

Shaped by

Deep pine-green walls, cream trim and carved wood. Every space at The Pinus is built to sit quietly among the deodars and make the most of the light and the view.

The cottages

Kashmiri style, two storeys

Bay windows, their own front steps and rooms on both floors. Ask us to keep families and groups together under one roof.

The lounge

The lawn

An open view of the peaks

Stone paths, garden seating and the bonfire area, with forested ridges rising straight up from the edge of the grass.

The place

Nunwan,
gateway to Pahalgam

The resort stands at Nunwan, on the approach road into Pahalgam and opposite the Yatra frisking point. It is close enough to the town for an easy evening in the market, and far enough to feel the quiet of the forest.

From here the roads lead up the valley to Aru and Betaab, and along the river towards Chandanwari. We are happy to help you plan each day.
