Who we are

Comfort of home,
thrill of the wild

At The Pinus Resort we believe a trip to Pahalgam should feel relaxed and homely. We put every effort into the comfort of our cottages, from cosy rooms to good, home-style food, and our team is always close at hand when you need something.

Travel can be tiring, so we try to make the rest easy. Once you arrive, all that is left is the rustle of the pine trees, the sound of the Lidder in the distance, the breeze off the mountains and the smell of the meadows.

Our motto

“A home in the heart of pines.”

We are dedicated to it every day, and we hope the charm of our cottages makes a home in our guests’ hearts too.

What we care about

The Pinus way

Homely, not hotel

A small resort run by people who know their guests by name, not by room number.

A team at your service

Our staff are on hand through the day for meals, hot water, bonfires and local advice.

Close to nature

Pine forest, an open lawn and mountain views, with the Lidder valley a short walk or drive away.

The place

Nunwan,
gateway to Pahalgam

The resort stands at Nunwan, on the approach road into Pahalgam and opposite the Yatra frisking point. It is close enough to the town for an easy evening in the market, and far enough to feel the quiet of the forest.

From here the roads lead up the valley to Aru and Betaab, and along the river towards Chandanwari. We are happy to help you plan each day.
