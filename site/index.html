Welcome to The Pinus

A quiet place
among the pines

The Pinus Resort sits in Nunwan, on the edge of Pahalgam, where the road meets the deodar forest and the Lidder river runs close by. Our cottages are panelled in warm pine, finished with carved wooden ceilings, and set on an open lawn that looks straight at the mountains.

We keep things simple and personal: comfortable rooms, good food, and a team that treats you like a guest in their own home. Your part is easy. Listen to the pines, walk the meadows, and sit by the fire in the evening.

Questions

Good to know

Where is The Pinus Resort?

The Pinus Resort is in Nunwan, Pahalgam, in Jammu and Kashmir, opposite the Yatra frisking point on the main road into Pahalgam. The PIN code is 192126.

How far is The Pinus Resort from Srinagar airport?

About 95 km by road, which usually takes 2.5 to 3 hours depending on the season and traffic. The resort is about 2.5 km before Pahalgam main market.

How do I book a room?

Send your dates through the enquiry form on the Reservation page, or call or WhatsApp +91 97977 80774. The team replies with availability and the rate. No payment is taken on the website.

What are the room rates?

Rates are on request because they change with the season. Send your dates and the team will reply with the current rate.

What is in the Deluxe Room?

Pine-panelled walls, a carved khatamband-style wooden ceiling, a double bed with an upholstered headboard, an electric kettle and an attached bathroom with a glass shower and a hot water geyser.

Is there a bonfire at the resort?

Yes. There is a bonfire area on the lawn. Let the team know in the afternoon and it will be ready by dusk.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 pm and check-out is by 12:00 noon.

Is breakfast available?

Yes. Breakfast is served in continental, Asian or buffet style, and the on-site restaurant serves Indian food. Room service is also available.

Is there parking at the resort?

Yes, free private parking is available on site.

Are children and pets allowed?

Children of all ages are welcome, but cots and extra beds are not available. Pets are not allowed.
