Plan your days

The valley on your doorstep

Pahalgam sits where two streams of the Lidder meet, surrounded by meadows and deodar forest. Some of the best of it is right at the resort; the rest is a short drive up the valley. Ask at the desk and we will help you plan.

01 · At the resort

Bonfire evenings

When the sun drops behind the ridge, the air turns cool fast. Our bonfire area on the lawn is the place to gather with family and friends under the stars. Let us know in the afternoon and we will have it ready by dusk.

On the lawnEvenings, on request

02 · Pine forests

Bask in the beauty

Take slow walks through the pine and deodar forest and along the rustic trails by the river. Early morning is best, when the mist is still in the trees and the meadows are quiet.

Easy walks1 to 3 hours

03 · On the Lidder

Reel in the fun

The cold, clear Lidder is known for its brown trout. Spend a peaceful few hours on the bank with a rod and line. Angling here needs a permit from the Jammu and Kashmir Fisheries Department, and we can point you to the right office.

Brown troutPermit required

04 · Day trip

Aru Valley

A drive up the valley to a village of wide meadows and forest. Aru is also the starting point for longer treks, such as those to the Kolahoi Glacier and the Tarsar Lake.

About 14 km from the resortHalf or full day

05 · Day trip

Betaab Valley

A green valley floor with the Lidder running through it, named after the 1983 Hindi film Betaab that was shot here. It lies on the road to Chandanwari, so many guests visit both on the same day.

About 17 km from the resortCombine with Chandanwari

06 · In winter

Pahalgam in the snow

In the winter months the valley turns white. Snow walks, snowball fights on the lawn and the quiet that only fresh snow brings. Road conditions change quickly in winter, so check with us before you travel.

Questions

Good to know

How far is Aru Valley from The Pinus Resort?

About 14 km by road. Aru is also the starting point for treks to the Kolahoi Glacier and the Tarsar Lake.

How far is Betaab Valley from The Pinus Resort?

About 17 km by road, on the way to Chandanwari, so many guests visit both on the same day.

Do I need a permit to fish for trout in the Lidder?

Yes. Angling in the Lidder needs a permit from the Jammu and Kashmir Fisheries Department. The resort team can point you to the right office.

Does Pahalgam get snow in winter?

Yes, the valley usually gets snow in the winter months. Road conditions change quickly, so check with the resort before travelling.

Can the resort help plan day trips?

Yes. The team can help you plan day trips to Aru, Betaab Valley and Chandanwari and tell you what is open in the season you visit.
