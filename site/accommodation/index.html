Sleep in the Pines

Small on purpose

The Pinus is a handful of cottages rather than a big hotel block, so every stay feels personal. Rooms are lined floor to ceiling in pine under carved wooden ceilings, each with its own bathroom and plenty of hot water, and a team close at hand for whatever you need.

Our signature room · Rates on request

Deluxe Room

Our signature room: walls of honey-coloured pine, a carved wooden ceiling in the Kashmiri khatamband style, a double bed with an upholstered headboard and soft white linen. The attached bathroom has a glass shower, a geyser for hot water and fresh towels.

The Cottages

Two-storey houses in deep pine green and cream, built to sit quietly among the deodars, each with bay windows, its own front steps and rooms on both floors. Travelling as a family or a group of friends? Tell us how many you are and we will try to keep you together under one roof.

Carved ceilings, deep sofas

The Lounge

A wood-panelled sitting room with deep sofas, brass lanterns and the same carved ceiling as the bedrooms. It is where guests gather for tea on a rainy afternoon or a game of cards after dinner, and where the day's plans for Aru or Betaab usually get made.
